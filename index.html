Our Philosophy

Wholesome Flavors, Pure Comfort.

The Basil Deck Street (थे बेसिल डेक स्ट्रीट) was created with a heartfelt culinary vision: to serve genuine vegetarian comfort food that satisfies every craving, at any hour of the day.

Conveniently situated along VIIT College Road in Kondhwa Budruk, Pune, our kitchen brings together a vibrant culinary landscape under one roof. Whether you begin your morning with piping hot Upma, authentic Tatte Idli, and TBD Special Chole Bhature, enjoy an evening Chaat or Pav Bhaji with friends, or gather the family for rich Paneer Amritsari and freshly baked Cheese Garlic Naan from our tandoor, every recipe is made using carefully sourced, fresh vegetarian ingredients.
